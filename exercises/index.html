Before the demonstration

Quick checklist

The marker will ask you to explain the code, not just show the result. Practise these six points.

1. Explain the coordinate system

SVG starts at (0, 0) in the top-left. x grows right, y grows down. The house rectangle at x=280 y=260 starts 280 units from the left and 260 from the top.

2. Explain the window group

Both windows are inside <g class="window-group" transform="translate(320 310)">. The group moves both windows together and shares their styling.

3. Explain the data join

selectAll("g.bar-row").data(data).join("g") creates one group per brand. The group is translated to the band scale position, then a rectangle and labels are appended to it.

4. Explain the scales

scaleLinear maps counts to width. scaleBand divides the height between the 25 brands and adds padding so the bars do not touch.

5. Show the evidence

Show the annotated SVG screenshot, the before/after pair, the DOM screenshot and the responsive chart. Resize the browser to prove the viewBox is doing its job.

6. Acknowledge AI use

The AI note is on the Exercise 4.1 page and in the README. Be ready to explain which parts were drafted with AI and how you customised and verified them.
